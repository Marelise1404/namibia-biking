Welcome

More Than
Just A Ride

Namibia Biking is a national platform created to introduce, connect and celebrate Namibia's motorcycle community.

From established motorcycle clubs and independent riders to charity organisations, businesses and event organisers — this is a place where the Namibian biking community can be discovered.

Our aim is to preserve our history, celebrate our present and create a record for future generations of Namibian bikers.
